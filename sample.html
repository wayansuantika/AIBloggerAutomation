Explore Udara Bali Yoga Detox & Spa: Oceanfront Wellness Retreat Guide

Udara Bali Yoga Detox & Spa is an oceanfront wellness sanctuary positioned directly along Seseh Beach in Cemagi, Badung, Bali. Designed with natural organic bamboo architecture, this peaceful retreat offers a holistic space for visitors to relax and recharge through schedule-driven yoga practices, tailored detox programs, spa therapies, and ocean-view dining.

Yoga Facilities & Holistic Offerings

The resort is built around multiple open-air wooden and bamboo shalas that overlook the ocean, creating an immersive setting for wellness practices:

  • Daily Yoga Classes: Features structured daily sessions covering Aerial Yoga, Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin Yoga, Pranayama, and Sound Healing.
  • Detox & Retreat Programs: Offers structured multi-day detox packages featuring herbal cleansing, hydrotherapy, and personalized wellness guidance.
  • Spa & Hydrotherapy Facilities: Outfitted with hot and cold plunge pools, a herbal steam room, a Finnish sauna, a Jacuzzi, and a dedicated meditation cave.
  • Community Events: Hosts regular community events including Ecstatic Dance, Sound Bath sessions, and Sunday wellness gatherings.

On-Site Dining: Organic Ocean Restaurant

The sanctuary houses an on-site dining establishment, Organic Ocean by Udara Bali, which focuses on nutrient-dense meals made from fresh, organic ingredients. The menu caters to various dietary preferences, offering vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and fresh seafood options.

Location & Connectivity

Situated in the quiet coastal area of Cemagi right next to Seseh Beach, Udara Bali provides an escape from heavy street traffic while maintaining easy connectivity:

  • Tanah Lot Temple: Approximately 4.2 km away.
  • Canggu Area: Roughly a 15-to-20-minute drive from central Canggu hubs like Echo Beach and Pererenan.
  • Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS): Located approximately 22 km away, reachable in about 50 to 60 minutes by car.
